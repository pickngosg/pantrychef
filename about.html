PantryChef SG

PantryChef SG is a free kitchen helper for people in Singapore. It keeps track of what is in your fridge and cupboard, tells you what to cook first before it expires, and helps you spend less on groceries.

Open the app

What it does

  • Pantry and expiry: list what you have and see what to use up first.
  • Recipes: save recipes, or import them from a link or a video. Works with English, Malay and Chinese.
  • Meal planner: plan your week and see calories and nutrition.
  • Shopping list and prices: build a list and compare prices at Singapore supermarkets.
  • Share with your household: up to 10 people can share one pantry, list and recipes.

Your data

You sign in with Google or an email address so your kitchen is saved and works on all your devices. We only use what you add to run the app. Read how we handle your information in our Terms and Privacy page. PantryChef SG is not made for children.
