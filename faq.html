PantryChef SG: questions and answers

PantryChef SG is a kitchen app for Singapore households, made by MAG-AMG Pte Ltd. Open the app · About · Privacy and terms

What is PantryChef SG?

PantryChef SG is a kitchen app made for Singapore households. You list what is in your fridge and pantry with expiry dates, and it helps you use things up, plan meals, build a shopping list and compare estimated shop prices.

Is PantryChef SG free?

The app is free to download and use. There is an optional paid plan called Plus, at S$3.98 a month or S$29.90 a year, with higher limits. Plus raises the monthly limits for AI video imports (40) and receipt or photo scans (60), compared with 3 of each on the free plan. Plus is bought on the website, not inside the Android app.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not at the moment. iPhone users can open pantrychefpro.com in Safari and add it to the Home Screen. The Android app is on Google Play.

Where do I get the Android app?

Search for PantryChef SG on Google Play, or open https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.pantrychefsg.app

Are the shop prices live?

No. Prices come from the app's own shop price list and each one shows the date it was checked. They are estimates to help you compare, not today's shelf prices. Shops can change prices at any time.

Is PantryChef SG linked to any supermarket?

No. It is made by MAG-AMG Pte Ltd and is not affiliated with any shop.

How do I add a recipe?

You can type your own, paste a link to a recipe web page, paste a video link (for example TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or YouTube), or use a photo. Imported recipes are read by AI, so please check them before cooking.

How does the expiry reminder work?

The app shows what expires soon and, on Android, can send a daily reminder to use these items up. You can turn reminders off in your phone settings.

Can my family share one pantry?

Yes. Household sharing lets up to 10 people share one pantry, shopping list and recipes.

Can I scan a receipt or a photo?

Yes. The app can read a photo to help add items. The free plan includes 3 scans a month and Plus includes 60.

Does it work offline?

It is built to keep working when your connection is weak, and your data syncs after you sign in again. Some features, such as imports, scans and price lookups, need internet.

What happens to my data?

Your pantry and recipes are stored in your account. You can delete your account inside the app. Read the privacy page for details.

How do I delete my account?

Open My account in the app and choose Delete account.

Is the daily intake tracker medical advice?

No. It is for personal tracking only and is not medical or nutrition advice.

How do I contact support?
