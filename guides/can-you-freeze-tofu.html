Can you freeze tofu?

By PantryChef SG · Updated 1 November 2026

Short answer: Yes, you can freeze tofu. Freezing makes it firmer, chewier and more spongy, which is good for stir-fries and soaking up sauce. Silken tofu does not freeze well because it turns grainy.

How to freeze tofu

Drain the tofu and pat it dry. Cut it into slabs or cubes if you like.

Put it in an airtight container or freezer bag, and add the date.

Freeze at -18°C or colder.

What happens to the texture

Water inside the tofu turns to ice and leaves small holes. After thawing, the tofu is chewy and spongy. Firm tofu works best. Silken or soft tofu tends to become grainy.

Thawing and cooking

Thaw in the fridge overnight, or in a sealed bag in cold water. Press out the water gently.

Use it in stir-fries, soups, braises and deep-fried dishes. It soaks up marinades well.

Tofu you have thawed in the fridge can be cooked as normal. Do not leave thawed tofu out for more than 2 hours.

Fridge storage

Keep unopened tofu chilled and follow the date on the pack. Once opened, tofu is commonly kept in fresh water in the fridge, changing the water daily, and used within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

Does frozen tofu taste different?

The taste is the same, but the texture is firmer and chewier.

How long can I keep tofu in the freezer?

Food stays safe indefinitely at -18°C, but for best quality use it within a couple of months.

Can I freeze tofu in its pack?

Many people do, but draining it first gives a better result.
