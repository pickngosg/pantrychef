How long can cooked chicken stay in the fridge?

By PantryChef SG · Updated 1 November 2026

Short answer: Cooked chicken and most leftovers keep for 3 to 4 days in a fridge at 4°C or colder. Chill them within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour in hot weather, and reheat until steaming hot.

The rule

USDA advises eating cooked leftovers, including chicken, within 3 to 4 days. Rice is the exception: see our rice guide, where the limit is shorter.

Chill them quickly

Do not leave cooked food out for more than 2 hours. If the room is hot (above 32°C), the limit is 1 hour. Singapore kitchens are often this warm.

Divide big pots into shallow containers so they cool faster.

Cover and refrigerate at 4°C or colder.

Reheating

Reheat until steaming hot throughout. USDA gives 74°C as the safe temperature for leftovers.

Reheat only what you will eat.

You can freeze cooked chicken if you will not eat it within 3 to 4 days.

Frequently asked questions

Can I eat cooked chicken after 5 days?

It is best not to. Food can be unsafe without smelling or looking bad, so follow the 3 to 4 day rule.

Is it safe to leave cooked chicken out overnight?

No. Throw it away if it has been out for more than 2 hours.

Can I freeze leftover chicken?

Yes. Freeze it within its 3 to 4 day fridge limit, ideally sooner.
