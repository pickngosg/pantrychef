How long can cooked rice stay in the fridge?

By PantryChef SG · Updated 1 November 2026

Short answer: Cooked rice is safest eaten within 1 day if you chill it within 1 hour of cooking. Some food agencies allow up to 2 days, but 1 day is the cautious rule. Reheat only once, until it is steaming hot all the way through.

Why rice needs extra care

Uncooked rice can carry spores of a bacterium called Bacillus cereus. The spores survive cooking. If cooked rice sits warm, they grow and can make a toxin. Reheating does not destroy that toxin, so the only real protection is cooling fast and storing cold.

The simple rules

Cool the rice within 1 hour. Spread it in a shallow container so it cools quickly, then cover and refrigerate.

Keep your fridge at 4°C or colder.

Eat chilled rice within 1 day (NHS advice). New Zealand's food regulator says cooled rice can be eaten within 2 days. If you want a single easy rule, use 1 day.

Reheat once only, until it is steaming hot throughout. Do not reheat the same rice twice.

In Singapore's heat

Do not leave cooked rice on the counter or in the rice cooker with the lid off to cool slowly. If food has been out for more than 2 hours, throw it away (USDA). In hot weather, 1 hour is a safer limit.

If you cook rice in bulk, portion it and freeze it. See the guide on freezing cooked rice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I reheat rice twice?

No. Reheat leftover rice only once, until steaming hot, and throw away any that is left over after that.

Can I tell by the smell if rice has gone bad?

Not reliably. The bacteria that make the toxin often do not change the smell or look, so follow the time limits instead.

Is 2 days in the fridge okay?

New Zealand's food regulator says cooled rice can be eaten within 2 days, while the NHS advises 1 day. Choosing 1 day is the more cautious option.
