How long do eggs last in the fridge?

By PantryChef SG · Updated 1 November 2026

Short answer: Raw eggs in their shell usually keep for about 3 to 5 weeks in the fridge, and hard-boiled eggs for about 1 week. Keep them in the carton at 4°C or colder, and always check the date printed on the pack.

Raw eggs

USDA guidance is that raw eggs in the shell keep for about 3 to 5 weeks in the fridge. Use the date printed on your carton as the first guide. In Singapore, "best before" is about quality, so check the egg when you crack it.

Store eggs in their carton on a shelf, not in the door, where the temperature changes more. Keep the fridge at 4°C or colder.

Hard-boiled eggs

Peeled or unpeeled, hard-boiled eggs should be eaten within about 1 week. Chill them soon after cooking. Do not leave them out for more than 2 hours.

Egg safety basics

Throw away eggs with cracked shells, or any egg that smells bad when cracked.

Wash your hands and the surfaces after handling raw eggs.

Cook eggs until the white and yolk are firm if you are pregnant, elderly, a young child or have a weak immune system.

Frequently asked questions

Should eggs be kept in the fridge in Singapore?

Yes. With Singapore's warm weather, a fridge at 4°C or colder is the safer place, and it keeps eggs fresh longer.

What does best before on an egg carton mean?

It is about quality. SFA explains that "best before" is a quality date, while "use by" is a safety date for highly perishable food. See our guide on use by and best before.

How long do boiled eggs last?

About 1 week in the fridge.
