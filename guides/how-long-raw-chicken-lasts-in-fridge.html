How long can raw chicken stay in the fridge?

By PantryChef SG · Updated 1 November 2026

Short answer: Raw chicken keeps for 1 to 2 days in a fridge at 4°C or colder. If you will not cook it by then, freeze it. Store it on the bottom shelf in a sealed container so juices cannot drip onto other food.

The rule

USDA says raw chicken should be cooked or frozen within 1 to 2 days of buying. Count from the day you bring it home, and check any "use by" date on the pack. Never eat chicken after its "use by" date.

How to store it

Put the pack in a container or on a plate on the bottom shelf, so raw juices cannot touch ready-to-eat food.

Keep the fridge at 4°C or colder.

Get it home quickly. In hot weather, do not leave chicken out of the fridge for more than 1 hour.

Do not rinse raw chicken. Rinsing splashes bacteria around your sink. Cooking kills them.

Freezing

Freeze chicken before the 1 to 2 day limit ends. Wrap tightly or use a freezer bag, label the date, and thaw it in the fridge, not on the counter.

Frequently asked questions

Can I eat raw chicken after the use by date?

No. "Use by" is a safety date for highly perishable food. Throw it away after that date.

How long can raw chicken stay out?

Throw away raw chicken left out for more than 2 hours, or more than 1 hour in hot weather above 32°C.

Should I wash chicken before cooking?

No. Washing spreads bacteria. Cook it thoroughly instead.
