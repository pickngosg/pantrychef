How to freeze cooked rice

By PantryChef SG · Updated 1 November 2026

Short answer: Cool cooked rice within 1 hour, pack it in flat single-meal portions, and freeze. Reheat from frozen until steaming hot. Food kept at -18°C is safe, but rice tastes best if you use it within a month or two.

Step by step

1. Cool fast. Spread hot rice in a shallow tray for up to 1 hour. Do not put a big hot pot in the freezer.

2. Portion. Put one meal's worth in a freezer bag or airtight box. Press bags flat so they freeze quickly and stack neatly.

3. Label. Write the date on the bag.

4. Freeze at -18°C or colder.

How long does it keep?

Food kept at -18°C stays safe indefinitely according to USDA, because freezing stops bacteria growing. Quality is what drops over time. As a practical rule, aim to use frozen rice within 1 to 2 months for the best texture.

Reheating

Reheat from frozen in the microwave with a splash of water, or in a pan. Make sure it is steaming hot all the way through.

Once you have reheated rice, eat it. Do not chill and reheat it again.

If you thaw rice in the fridge first, eat it within 1 day.

Frequently asked questions

Can I freeze rice that has been in the fridge?

Only if it was chilled within 1 hour of cooking and is still within its 1 day fridge limit. Freeze it as soon as possible rather than at the end of that time.

Do I need to thaw frozen rice first?

No. You can reheat it straight from frozen, as long as it gets steaming hot all through.

Why does reheated frozen rice sometimes dry out?

Rice loses moisture in the freezer. Adding a small splash of water before microwaving helps.
