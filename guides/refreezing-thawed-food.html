Can you refreeze thawed food?

By PantryChef SG · Updated 1 November 2026

Short answer: You can safely refreeze food that was thawed in the fridge, though quality may drop. If it was thawed in cold water or the microwave, cook it first before refreezing. Throw away anything left out for more than 2 hours (1 hour in hot weather).

The rule of thumb

Thawed in the fridge: safe to refreeze without cooking, according to USDA. You may notice a loss of quality because thawing and freezing break down moisture.

Thawed in cold water or the microwave: cook it first, then you can freeze the cooked food.

Left out for more than 2 hours (1 hour above 32°C): throw it away. Do not refreeze.

Safe ways to thaw

In the fridge. This is the safest and lets you refreeze later.

In a sealed bag under cold running water or in cold water that you change often.

In the microwave, then cook straight away.

Never thaw on the kitchen counter.

Cooked food

If you cooked raw frozen food, you can freeze the cooked leftovers. Do the same cooling and storing steps as for any leftovers.

Freezer temperature of -18°C keeps food safe indefinitely, but repeated freezing makes texture worse.

Frequently asked questions

Can I refreeze chicken I thawed in the fridge?

Yes, it is safe, though the texture may be a little drier. Use it soon.

What if my freezer stopped working for a few hours?

If food still has ice crystals or feels as cold as in a fridge, you can refreeze it. If it has been above 4°C for more than 2 hours, throw it away.

Can I refreeze ice cream?

It is best not to. Once ice cream has melted, bacteria can grow, and refrozen ice cream also turns icy and grainy. Throw it away if it melted fully.
