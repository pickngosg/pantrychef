How to store kangkong and leafy vegetables

By PantryChef SG · Updated 1 November 2026

Short answer: Keep leafy vegetables dry, loosely wrapped in paper, and in the fridge chiller. Do not wash them until you cook. Most last 2 to 4 days this way, and kangkong is one of the quickest to wilt, so cook it first.

Five simple steps

1. Do not wash before storing. Extra water speeds up rot.

2. Remove damaged or yellow leaves.

3. Wrap the stems in dry paper towel or newspaper.

4. Put them in a loose bag or a box with the lid slightly open, so moisture can escape.

5. Store in the fridge chiller and use within 2 to 4 days.

Kangkong specifically

Kangkong has hollow stems and loses water fast. Cook it first in your week's plan. If it has wilted but not turned slimy, soak it in cold water for 10 to 15 minutes to perk it up.

When to throw it away

Slimy leaves, a sour smell or widespread black spots mean it is time to bin it.

Wash all vegetables well just before cooking.

Frequently asked questions

Should I wash vegetables before putting them in the fridge?

No. Wash just before cooking. Damp leaves spoil faster.

Can I freeze leafy vegetables?

You can freeze them after a quick blanch, but they become soft. They suit soups and stir-fried dishes better than salads.

Why does kangkong go slimy so quickly?

It has a high water content and thin leaves, so trapped moisture and warmth speed up spoilage.
