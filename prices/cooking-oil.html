Cooking oil prices in Singapore

By PantryChef SG · Price snapshot: 1 November 2026

Short answer: On 1 November 2026, the cheapest 2 litre bottle of vegetable, canola or sunflower oil we found was at FairPrice: $2.48 per litre ($4.95 for Smart Choice Cooking Oil - Vegetable 2L). Across 4 shops the lowest price ranged from $2.48 to $3.95 per litre.

Money-saving tip

Vegetable oil is usually cheaper than canola or sunflower oil. Compare per litre if you also buy 1 litre bottles.

How we compare

We take product listings from 4 Singapore supermarkets, keep only comparable items, and convert each to a price per litre. "Lowest" is the cheapest product that matched. "Typical" is the median of all matched products in that shop.

Frequently asked questions

Where is 2 litre bottle of vegetable, canola or sunflower oil cheapest in Singapore?

On 1 November 2026 the lowest price we found was at FairPrice, at $2.48 per litre. Prices change often, so check the shop before you buy.

How often are these prices updated?

We update this page regularly from a snapshot of shop listings. This one is dated 1 November 2026. Shop prices and promotions change daily.

Is 2 litres cheaper per litre than 1 litre?

Often yes, but check. Promotions can make a 1 litre bottle cheaper per litre than the 2 litre one.
