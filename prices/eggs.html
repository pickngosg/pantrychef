Egg prices in Singapore

By PantryChef SG · Price snapshot: 1 November 2026

Short answer: On 1 November 2026, the cheapest 10-egg pack of ordinary chicken eggs we found was at Sheng Siong: $0.24 per egg ($2.35 for Dasoon First Born Eggs (10 pcs) 450 g). Across 4 shops the lowest price ranged from $0.24 to $0.39 per egg.

Money-saving tip

Pack size and brand change the price a lot. Compare the price per egg, not just the pack price.

How we compare

We take product listings from 4 Singapore supermarkets, keep only comparable items, and convert each to a price per egg. "Lowest" is the cheapest product that matched. "Typical" is the median of all matched products in that shop.

Frequently asked questions

Where is 10-egg pack of ordinary chicken eggs cheapest in Singapore?

On 1 November 2026 the lowest price we found was at Sheng Siong, at $0.24 per egg. Prices change often, so check the shop before you buy.
