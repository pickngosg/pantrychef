Fresh milk prices in Singapore

By PantryChef SG · Price snapshot: 1 November 2026

Short answer: On 1 November 2026, the cheapest 1 litre carton of plain fresh milk we found was at RedMart: $3.02 per litre ($3.02 for Magnolia UHT Fresh Milk 1L). Across 3 shops the lowest price ranged from $3.02 to $3.51 per litre.

Money-saving tip

Imported Japanese milk costs about double the everyday brands. Compare the same type when you check shops.

How we compare

We take product listings from 3 Singapore supermarkets, keep only comparable items, and convert each to a price per litre. "Lowest" is the cheapest product that matched. "Typical" is the median of all matched products in that shop.

Frequently asked questions

Where is 1 litre carton of plain fresh milk cheapest in Singapore?

On 1 November 2026 the lowest price we found was at RedMart, at $3.02 per litre. Prices change often, so check the shop before you buy.

How often are these prices updated?

We update this page regularly from a snapshot of shop listings. This one is dated 1 November 2026. Shop prices and promotions change daily.

Does "fresh milk" mean chilled milk?

Not always. Some products labelled fresh milk are UHT and keep unopened on the shelf. Check the pack.
