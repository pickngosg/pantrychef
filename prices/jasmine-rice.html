Jasmine (fragrant) rice prices in Singapore

By PantryChef SG · Price snapshot: 1 November 2026

Short answer: On 1 November 2026, the cheapest 5 kg bag of Thai fragrant or jasmine rice we found was at Sheng Siong: $1.50 per kg ($7.50 for Happy Family Special Fragrant Rice 5 kg). Across 4 shops the lowest price ranged from $1.50 to $2.38 per kg.

Money-saving tip

Premium Hom Mali brands often cost about double the cheapest fragrant rice. Compare price per kg to judge a bag.

How we compare

We take product listings from 4 Singapore supermarkets, keep only comparable items, and convert each to a price per kg. "Lowest" is the cheapest product that matched. "Typical" is the median of all matched products in that shop.
