Terms and Privacy

Last updated: 22 October 2026. Contact: cook@pantrychefpro.app

What PantryChef SG is

PantryChef SG helps you track your kitchen, plan meals and keep recipes. It is provided as it is, and we work to keep it running well but cannot promise it will always be available or error free.

What we keep

Your email address, the pantry, recipes, meal plan and cart you sync, your plan and how many video imports and receipt scans you have used, and the links or text you submit to import a recipe. If you do not sign in, your data stays on your own device.

How we use it

To run the app, sync your devices, read recipes from links you share, and manage your plan. We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Who helps us

Supabase stores your account and synced data. Google's AI reads the recipe links, videos and text you submit. Apify helps read public Instagram posts. Stripe handles payments, and we never see or store your full card number. These providers may process your information on servers outside Singapore.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account yourself in Backup & Sync with Delete my account. This removes your account and synced data and cancels Plus straight away. Payment records stay with Stripe, as the law requires. You can also ask us to see, correct or stop using your data using the contact below, in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act.

Plus subscription

Plus costs S$3.98 a month or S$29.90 a year. It renews automatically until you cancel. A free trial, if offered, turns into a paid plan unless you cancel before it ends. Cancel any time with Manage plan; you keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for. If you are not satisfied, contact us within 14 days of your first payment and we will review a refund.

Please check for yourself

The Halal check, nutrition numbers, prices and recipes read by AI are guides only and can be wrong. Always check ingredients, allergens and Halal certification yourself. This app is not medical or dietary advice.

Changes

We may update these terms. If you keep using the app after a change, you accept it.
