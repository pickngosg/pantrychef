Terms and Privacy

Last updated: 14 November 2026. Contact: cook@pantrychefpro.com

Who we are

PantryChef SG is run by MAG-AMG Pte Ltd, a company in Singapore. In this page, “we” means MAG-AMG Pte Ltd. You can reach us at the contact email above for any question about your data.

What PantryChef SG is

PantryChef SG helps you track your kitchen, plan meals and keep recipes. It is provided as it is, and we work to keep it running well but cannot promise it will always be available or error free.

What we keep

Your email address, the pantry, recipes, meal plan and cart you sync, your plan and how many video imports and receipt scans you have used, and the links or text you submit to import a recipe. If you choose to share a price (for example from a receipt, or by typing what you paid), we also keep the shop, the food, the size, the price and the date. Other shoppers only see the middle price for each food and shop, never your name, email or photo. If you add a product that is not in our list, we keep its name, the shop, the pack size, the price and the date, and other shoppers can find it without seeing who added it. You need an account to use the app. Your pantry and recipes are saved on your own device first and then copied to our cloud, so they sync between your devices. If you turn on reminders, we also keep the details your phone gives us to send you notifications. We keep recipe pictures from posts you import, so they keep showing in your app. If you use a household, we keep your household code, the nicknames of its members and the shared items you put in it, which everyone in that household can see. If you answer the short polls in the app, we keep your answers and the date, without your name. To make repeat imports faster and cheaper, we also keep a shared copy of the recipe read from a public video link (the recipe and picture only, no personal details). We also count simple things people do in the app, such as opening it, adding a pantry item or saving a recipe, so we can see what works and what does not. These counts are linked to your account but never include the names or contents of what you add, and they are deleted after 90 days. If something goes wrong, the app may ask whether you want to send a problem report. A report is only sent if you tap Send, or if you choose Report a problem in My account. It includes your phone and browser type, the screen you were on, the error message and any note you write. It does not include your pantry, recipes or email. We keep a problem report until we have fixed the problem, and delete fixed reports after 6 months. If you use Ask AI, the app copies a list of your pantry items to your clipboard so you can paste it into an AI app that you choose. PantryChef SG does not send that list anywhere, and what you paste into another service is handled by that service under its own terms.

When you open our website, we add one to a visit count for that day and for where you came from (for example Google, Instagram or the app). We use no cookies and keep no IP address, account or other personal detail for this count, and we cannot tell who you are from it.

How we use it

To run the app, sync your devices, read recipes from links you share, and manage your plan. We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

Who helps us

Supabase stores your account and synced data. Google's AI reads the recipe links, videos, text and photos (for example receipts or product labels) you submit. Apify helps read public Instagram, TikTok and Facebook posts. Stripe handles payments, and we never see or store your full card number. When we read a recipe web page for you, our own server opens it. If the website blocks our server, the web address of that page may be sent to a reader service (Jina AI) so it can open the page for us. We no longer use free public proxy websites for this. These providers may process your information on servers outside Singapore. We pick providers that protect your data, and we only share what is needed to do the job.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account yourself in My account with Delete my account. This removes your account and synced data and cancels Plus straight away. Payment records stay with Stripe, as the law requires. To delete your account without opening the app, email us at the contact above from the email address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will delete it and tell you when it is done. You can also ask us to see, correct or stop using your data using the contact below, in line with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act.

Plus subscription

Plus is sold on our website. The Android app does not sell subscriptions. Plus costs S$3.98 a month or S$29.90 a year. It renews automatically until you cancel. A free trial, if offered, turns into a paid plan unless you cancel before it ends. Cancel any time with Manage plan; you keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for. If you are not satisfied, contact us within 14 days of your first payment and we will review a refund.

Please check for yourself

The Halal check, nutrition numbers, prices and recipes read by AI are guides only and can be wrong. Always check ingredients, allergens and Halal certification yourself. This app is not medical or dietary advice.
